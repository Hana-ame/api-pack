---
name: zoom-out
description: Provides broader context or a higher-level architectural view on an unfamiliar section of code. Use when you need to understand how a piece of code fits into the bigger picture.
---

Give me a zoomed-out architectural view of this code:

- Where does this fit in the overall system?
- What are the key abstractions and their relationships?
- What patterns are being used?
- What are the data flows (inputs, processing, outputs)?
- What are the boundaries and interfaces to other components?

Start with the highest-level view, then drill down one layer at a time only if needed.
